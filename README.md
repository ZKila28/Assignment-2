Project title
Lae Urban Youths Bike Workshop

Purpose
A three-page informational website for a volunteer-run community bike workshop.
Visitors can learn about the workshop, view services, course dates and prices,find opening hours and location, and book a repair stand, register for a courseor arrange a bike donation through an online form.

Target audience
Local adult riders, parents of young riders, students and budget-conscious commuters in the Lae Urban area.

Key user tasks
Find out what the workshop offers and what it costs (services page).
Check opening hours and how to get there (contact page).
Book a repair stand or register for a maintenance course (contact page form).
Donate a bike or volunteer (home and services pages, actioned via the form).
Understand what a first visit involves (home page).
